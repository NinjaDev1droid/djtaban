My Journey

Music has always been the language I understood best. Growing up surrounded by the sounds of Africa — the rhythm of Afrobeats, the deep groove of Amapiano, the energy of Hip-Hop — DJing wasn't a career choice. It was inevitable.

"I don't just play music. I read the room, feel the crowd, and take them on a journey they didn't know they needed."

I started behind the decks with nothing but a laptop, a controller, and an obsession with music. Event by event, set by set, I developed my style — a sound that crosses genres and borders, built on the foundation of African rhythm but fluent in every language the dance floor speaks.

Today I play clubs, weddings, rooftop events, festivals, and private gatherings across Tel Aviv and beyond. Every set is different. Every crowd teaches me something new. That's what keeps this alive.
